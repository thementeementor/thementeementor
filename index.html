Questions students ask first

Which classes do you teach?

Zoology for NEET across the Class 11 and Class 12 syllabus, plus dedicated revision and test batches for repeaters.

Is NCERT enough for NEET Zoology?

NCERT is the base of almost every question. We read it line by line, then add diagrams, comparisons and MCQ practice so you can apply it, not just recall it.

How do I get my doubts solved?

Bring them to the doubt-support sessions or send them through the contact form. Each doubt is answered with a diagram or worked example when it helps.

Will I know how I am performing?

Every test comes with a topic-wise report that shows where marks were lost and what to revise next.

Can I join in the middle of the year?

Yes. Recorded classes and chapter notes help you catch up, and the foundation recap sessions bring you level with the batch.
